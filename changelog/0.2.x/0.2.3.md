# 0.2.3 — 2026-11-19

## Added

- **`usda_get_food` and `usda_get_foods`** take `provenance` (default `false`); each nutrient then carries `derivation` (code, description, source), `dataPoints`, `min`, `max`, and `median`, each only where FDC sends it, and the response is two to three times as large ([#50](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/50)).
- **`usda_search_foods`** takes `pageSize` up to 200; a larger value is served at 200 with a notice, and `USDA_CLAMP_LIMITS=false` rejects it ([#51](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/51)).
- **`usda_list_nutrients`** takes `query`, a case-insensitive `name` substring or an exact `id` or `number`, within `category`; no match returns `nutrients: []` and a notice ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/49)).

## Fixed

- **The nutrient table** behind `usda_list_nutrients` and `usda://nutrients` holds 269 rows, where 0.2.2's held 147: a row for every nutrient Foundation, SR Legacy, and Survey (FNDDS) foods report and for each one a sample of 8,310 Branded foods reports, 1062 (Energy, kJ) and 2033 (AOAC 2011.25 fiber) among the new ones ([#49](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/49)).
- **A non-transient refetch failure** ends a timed-out batch's per-ID split at once: queued IDs are not sent, in-flight refetches abort, and the call fails with that error ([#46](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/46)).
- **The 15 s attempt bound** of a batch or search page no longer cuts a response body FDC has started sending: it covers the wait for headers, and the body gets its own 15 s ([#47](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/47)).
- **`usda_compare_foods`** drops repeated entries from `fdcIds` and `nutrients`, compares the first 5 distinct IDs, and returns the rest in `deferredIds` with a notice naming the follow-up call (the same `nutrients`, `quantity`, and `unit`; a single leftover ID is paired with one already compared); `fdcIds` takes up to 500 entries, and `USDA_CLAMP_LIMITS=false` still rejects more than 5. Its failure messages name every deferred ID. `fdcIds` holding fewer than 2 distinct IDs (`[X, X]`), or one distinct ID plus IDs FDC has no data for (`[X, X, unknown]`), now fails `too_few_foods`; 0.2.2 returned a comparison of the food with itself ([#52](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/52)).
- **Detail units and `publicationDate`** match search and the nutrient reference: `unit` on `usda_get_food`, `usda_get_foods`, `usda_compare_foods`, and `usda://food/{fdcId}` is `KCAL`, `G`, `MG`, or `UG` where it was `kcal`, `g`, `mg`, or `µg`, and `publicationDate` on `usda_get_food` and `usda://food/{fdcId}` is `YYYY-MM-DD` where it was `M/D/YYYY` ([#53](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/53)).
- **`usda://food/{fdcId}`** logs a bad or unknown ID at `notice` instead of `error`; the JSON-RPC errors are unchanged ([#54](https://github.com/cyanheads/usda-mcp-server/issues/54)).
- **The `usda_search_foods` preview** keeps nutrient 2033; 0.2.2's kept eight nutrient IDs and left it out, so a Foundation food that reports fiber only as 2033 showed no fiber.
- **`content[]`** escapes the nutrient `unit`, `dataType`, the SR `number`, `publicationDate`, and `publishedDate` as it does names and descriptions; 0.2.2 wrote them as FDC sent them. No value FDC sends today changes, and `structuredContent` is untouched.
- **The `usda_get_foods` deferral notice** names the `nutrients` and `provenance` to carry into the follow-up call ("the same nutrients and provenance"); 0.2.2's text named only the IDs, so a follow-up sent as written returned full profiles.
- **`usda_get_food` and `usda_get_foods` `content[]`** leave out the `SR#` segment for a nutrient with no legacy number; 0.2.2 wrote `SR#: ` followed by nothing. `structuredContent` is unchanged.
